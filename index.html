Data Safety & Account Deletion

Last Updated: October 2026
Sages is designed to provide personalized AI guidance while giving you control over your account and personal information. This page explains the types of data used by Sages, the services involved in processing it, and how you can request deletion of your account.

1. Information Used by Sages

Sages processes information that is necessary to provide account functionality, personalized conversations, continuity across sessions, subscriptions, notifications, and application reliability.

  • Account information: information such as your email address and profile/display information used to create and authenticate your Sages account.
  • Conversations: messages exchanged with individual mentors and Council conversations.
  • Personalization data: information stored in Sages features such as Memories, Threads, Goals, Decisions, and Reflections.
  • Preferences and settings: settings used to personalize your Sages experience, such as applicable reflection and account preferences.
  • Subscription and usage information: information needed to determine Premium entitlement, subscription status, usage limits, and access to paid features.
  • Notification information: device notification tokens and related device-registration information used to deliver notifications you are eligible to receive.
  • Technical and diagnostic information: crash reports and technical diagnostics used to detect errors, improve reliability, and maintain the application.
  • Support communications: information you provide when contacting Sages support.

2. Security

Data transmitted between the Sages application and supported backend services is protected in transit using HTTPS/TLS.

We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards designed to protect personal information. However, no online service can guarantee absolute security.

3. AI Processing

Sages uses artificial intelligence to generate mentor responses, Council responses, reflections, and other contextual features.

When an AI feature is used, the relevant message and selected contextual information may be sent to our AI service provider so that a response can be generated.

We do not intentionally send passwords or full payment-card information to the AI provider.

4. Service Providers

Sages does not sell your personal information. We use trusted service providers where necessary to operate the app. Depending on the feature you use, these providers may process relevant information on our behalf.

  • Supabase: authentication, database, and backend data infrastructure.
  • OpenAI: processing relevant prompts and contextual information to provide AI-generated responses.
  • RevenueCat: subscription entitlement and purchase-status management.
  • Apple App Store and Google Play: processing subscription purchases, renewals, cancellations, and store billing.
  • Firebase: crash diagnostics and push-notification infrastructure.
  • DigitalOcean: hosting infrastructure used by the Sages backend.
  • Inngest: background workflow processing for supported Sages features.
  • Email infrastructure: processing support communications when you contact us.

5. Account and Data Deletion

You can request deletion of your Sages account at any time. Sages provides both an in-app deletion method and an out-of-app deletion request method.

Method A: Delete Your Account in the App

If you still have access to Sages:

  1. Open Sages and sign in to your account.
  2. Open Profile.
  3. Select Privacy & Security.
  4. Select Delete Account.
  5. Review the deletion notice and confirm your request.

Method B: Request Deletion Without the App

If you no longer have Sages installed or cannot access your account, you may request account deletion by contacting us from the email address associated with your Sages account.

Email: support@sagesapp.com
Subject: Account Deletion Request

We may ask you to verify account ownership before processing an out-of-app deletion request.

6. What Happens When You Delete Your Account?

Deleting your account permanently disables your Sages account and removes your ability to access the application using that account.

Account-associated information will be deleted or de-identified in accordance with the application's deletion process, subject to any information that must temporarily be retained for legitimate security, fraud-prevention, billing, legal, backup, or operational purposes.

Information retained for such purposes is not kept indefinitely and is handled according to applicable retention requirements.

Important: deleting your Sages account does not automatically cancel an active Apple App Store or Google Play subscription. If you do not want a subscription to renew, cancel it separately through your Apple or Google account subscription settings.

7. Clearing Conversation History

Sages also provides a separate option for deleting saved conversation history without deleting your entire account.

Clearing conversation history permanently removes saved conversations and their messages. Memories, Goals, Threads, Decisions, and Reflections are stored separately and are not automatically deleted when conversation history is cleared.

Some source links or contextual references associated with deleted conversations may also be removed.

8. Subscriptions and Payment Information

Sages does not directly process full payment-card details. Subscription payments are processed by the Apple App Store or Google Play.

RevenueCat is used to help Sages determine your current subscription entitlement and synchronize subscription status with the application.
