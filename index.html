Data Safety & Deletion

Last Updated: June 2026
Transparency is vital to the Sages platform. This page outlines exactly how your data is handled, secured, and how you can exercise your right to permanently delete your presence from our Council. This information is provided in compliance with Google Play Store Data Safety requirements.

1. Data Collection and Security

To provide a deeply personalized experience, we collect specific data points. All data transmitted between your device and our servers is encrypted in transit using secure, modern cryptographic protocols (HTTPS/TLS).

  • Personal Info: Name and Email Address (used strictly for account authentication via Supabase).
  • App Activity: Your chat history and interactions with the Sages (used to maintain continuity in your mentorship).
  • App Performance: Crash logs and diagnostics via Firebase (used to ensure the stability of the platform).

2. Data Sharing Practices

We do not sell your personal data. However, to function as an advanced mind platform, certain data must be processed by trusted third-party sentries:

  • OpenAI: Receives the text of your prompts to generate the mentor's responses. Your name, email, and personal identifiers are strictly withheld from this process.
  • Google Firebase: Receives anonymized crash reports and usage analytics to help us improve the application architecture.

3. Account and Data Deletion

You maintain full sovereignty over your data. If you choose to depart, you may permanently delete your account and all associated data at any time. We offer two methods for deletion:

Method A: In-App Deletion

If you currently have the app installed on your device, you can delete your data immediately by following these steps:

  1. Open the Sages application and log in.
  2. Navigate to Profile menu.
  3. Select Security & Privacy.
  4. Tap Relinquish Account and confirm your decision.

Method B: Out-of-App Deletion Request

If you have uninstalled the app and wish to delete your account without reinstalling it, please send an email from the address associated with your account to request immediate deletion:

Email: support@sagesapp.com
Subject: Account Deletion Request

4. What Happens Upon Deletion?

When an account deletion is triggered (either in-app or via an email request), the following actions occur immediately and irreversibly within our Supabase database:

  • Your email address and display name are permanently erased.
  • Your authentication credentials and access tokens are revoked.
  • Your entire "Council History" (all chat dialogues and stored interactions) is permanently wiped from our active servers.

Note: Routine secure server backups may retain an encrypted snapshot of data for up to 30 days before being automatically overwritten.
